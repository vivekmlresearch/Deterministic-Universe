# Privacy Policy — Deterministic Universe

Effective date: 27 September 2026

Deterministic Universe operates fully offline. It does not require an account, display advertising, request sensitive permissions, collect personal information, use analytics, or transmit graph and benchmark data. Preferences are stored only on the user's device. Android backup may copy preferences to the user's own backup account when enabled by the user.

For questions, replace this paragraph with the publisher's public support email before Play Store submission.
